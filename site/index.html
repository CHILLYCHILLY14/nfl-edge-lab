How to read this. Model edge compares the model with the complete no-vig two-way market; break-even and realized value at the offered price are kept separate. Every edge shown is compressed: raw edges are squeezed toward a ceiling of about 5%, because a home-made model claiming 15% against the NFL market has found a bug in itself, not an opportunity. Tiers scale with confidence, and BEST BET additionally requires the model to genuinely disagree with the market line rather than just with its price.
Nothing here is a guarantee. The model is one opinion priced against the sharpest market in North American sport. Check the Accuracy tab before trusting a number: if GOOD plays are not beating LEAN plays, the labels are decoration. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
